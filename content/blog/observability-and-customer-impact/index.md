# Human Centered Design

Human Centered Design (HCD) revolves around building products or services with the customer in mind.
Graphics and product designers create brands around the way they anticipate people will respond to seeing colors. Or how the name of a business will be perceived by
their target market as well as their competition. How will something feel in a user's hand? How does their brain respond to the flow of switching between screens or
apps.

These concepts are over my head from a technical perspective, but as an engineer they still make sense. When I build something, the end result should be the satisfaction
of the people supporting us, not the brand or the product itself. Although, when you live closely entwined with how something is built and you iterate over making
something better, it's hard to not see the product or your business as your "baby" that needs to be protected over the customer itself.

When building products, it's particularly easy to become attached to the nuts and bolts and make the people who break things the enemy. Stop breaking my shit!
But let's flip that on its head and say the people breaking your product are using it, which supports your lifestyle and keeps the lights on.

# Site Reliability Engineering

Site Reliability Engineering isn't something that I went to school for, or something I had been working towards. I stumbled upon an opportunity that interested me
in search of my next challenge. Honestly, I'm still figuring out what SRE means to me and my team. We spend the majority of our time as firefighters, working one problem
then the next. We're cloud engineers, systems engineers and software engineers all from different backgrounds and experiences. Some of us have spent decades with this
company solving the same problems we're solving today but with new technology.
